
# Maji Ndogo Power BI Project


### 1. Introduction

The Maji Ndogo project is a nationwide initiative aimed at improving access to safe drinking water across five provinces: Akatsi, Hawassa, Sokoto, Kilimani, and Amanzi. The dashboard provides a data-driven overview of progress, spending, coverage gaps, and distribution of improvement projects.

### 2. Purpose of the Report

* Monitor water infrastructure improvements
* Evaluate cost efficiency
* Analyze project reach
* Support evidence-based decision‑making
* Strengthen transparency

### 3. Goals of the Dashboard

* Measure progress in water access
* Visualize cost and project distribution geographically
* Recognize provincial funding disparities
* Compare cumulative cost vs. budget
* Assess timelines and completion patterns
* Identify high‑impact improvement types

### 4. Key Findings

**People Helped:** 4M people have benefited.

**Project Completion:** 22% completed, 78% remaining.

**Basic Water Access:** 48% of the country has basic access.

**Most Expensive Improvement Type:** Installing RO filters (~$10M+).

**Cost by Province:** Sokoto holds the largest share (~32%), followed by Kilimani, Akatsi, Hawassa, and Amanzi.

**Cumulative Cost:** $33.79M vs. $25.31M goal (33.51% over budget).

**Water Sources Tracked:** 19,910 sources.

**Geographic Distribution:** Strongest improvements in central and northern regions; weaker in the south.

### 5. Strategic Interpretation

* Progress is measurable but insufficient
* Certain provinces require urgent support
* Overspending requires procurement reforms
* Filtration systems are costly but necessary
* More time and resources required to reach nation‑wide coverage
